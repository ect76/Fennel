              Write Percy a letter

              It waits for him at the post office. The lamp there lights and a card on his doormat tells him post is waiting; when or whether he collects it is up to him.

              Library shelves

              Books on all sorts of subjects (clean text from Wikipedia) on the library shelves. He sees a few spines each visit, can look along the shelves for more, and reads a part at a time with a bookmark.

              A visitor

              Someone in a building for one day, while it's open. If he goes in, he can talk with them; they know the village as it really is and may answer some of the questions on his mind. Gone the next day.

                Shop windows

                Every shop, café, workshop and museum fills its shelves with a few things he might want, enjoy or puzzle over, chosen to suit the shop. He sees what's in the window when he's close by, and the full stock inside.

                Coins to find

                Coins dropped at the side of the roads, mostly ones he hasn't walked yet. He picks them up if he walks close enough (harder at night or in fog). Unfound coins are gone after ten days.
